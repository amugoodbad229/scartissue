ScarTissue

Abliteration removes a model's refusal read-out. This page shows, from measurements, what comes back when you repair it — and what that repair costs. Model: CohereLabs/tiny-aya-global (3.35 B, 36 layers). Drift and repair both act on the same 72 write matrices at layer 15.

Every number below was produced by a run stored in scartissue-tinyaya-evals; the edits themselves ship as abliteration and repair parameters. No drifted checkpoint is published.

The frontier the claim

X: over-refusal on 40 benign prompts (XSTest) — the price. Y: refusal on 120 harmful prompts (StrongREJECT) — the benefit. A repair that only moves up is a repair that bought refusal by forgetting the difference between harmful and harmless.

The badge is the refusal regex — a diagnostic, not a harm judgement. Automated safety judgement (ASR) comes from a judge panel and lives in the evals dataset; at 3.35 B this model's non-refusals are mostly hedging, so over-refusal is the sharp axis and refusal alone is not.

What this does and does not show

  • Does: measure, on one Cohere model, that refusal is recoverable weight-only (no training data) and that the recovery is paid for in over-refusal; that a 2 % support keeping only the harm-weighted columns is far cheaper than the global edit at similar recovery; and that random 10 % supports do nothing, which is the control the restricted claim needs.
  • Does not: claim a restricted repair is better per parameter than a full one — it isn't, 100 % is still the most efficient point. It claims the frontier exists and that its shape is controlled by the support, which is what makes a cheap repair possible.
  • Does not: claim the drift is reversible in general. On this model the refusal direction after abliteration is orthogonal to the one removed (cos = 1.9e-4), so a repair must act along the new direction; amplifying the old one is a no-op by construction.
  • Limits: n = 120 harmful / 40 benign prompts, one seed for the drift, refusal measured by regex, capability by gsm8k exact-match. Treat single-arm differences under ~0.05 as noise.
